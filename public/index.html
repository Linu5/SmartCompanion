About these results

Train departures and travel times use LTA’s published timetable, including its service calendars. They are scheduled times, not live predictions. Transfers allow four minutes; your actual walking time may differ.

Bus arrival predictions and occupancy come from LTA. Total bus journey times are calculated estimates: route distance at 18 km/h, plus 21 seconds per stop. Walking uses straight-line distance × 1.3 at your selected pace (default 75 m/min); check your actual pedestrian route. Your maximum walking distance applies at each end of a bus alternative.

Bus crowd levels use LTA’s occupancy categories: low = seats available; moderate = standing available; high = limited standing. These are categories, not passenger counts or percentages. Missing or old readings are labelled, and occupancy can change before boarding.

Train crowd levels describe stations, not individual train carriages. LTA updates them in ten-minute intervals. Missing or old data is labelled.

The time comparison uses LTA's daily station crowd forecast in 30-minute intervals. Each level describes your starting station at that time, not the whole train journey. A missing forecast is unavailable, never assumed quiet. Train comparisons include scheduled waiting and transfers, but not your trip to the station. Current service and lift notices are applied; recheck before travelling.

Nearby road alerts come from LTA Traffic Incidents within 1.5 km of the selected station or bus stop. They are current reports, not future traffic predictions or a measurement of congestion along your bus route. Historical passenger patterns describe tap-in/out activity, not traffic speed; they do not replace official crowd forecasts. No reported incident does not mean roads are clear.

Bus only finds direct services. Train & bus also checks walking and bus connections to rail. It checks a limited set of nearby transfer options. Automatic uses 1 km at each end, or 450 m with “Less walking”. Crowd filters only accept upcoming arrivals with matching fresh readings; “Avoid high crowding” accepts seats or standing space, and “Seats reported available” accepts seats only. This may leave no matching arrivals. Standalone bus-to-bus transfers are not included; connected journeys can include bus → train → bus. Diversions may change the published route.

Map lines connect station or stop coordinates. They are schematic connections, not exact tracks or roads.

Wheelchair access filters bus predictions by LTA’s wheelchair-accessible bus flag. It also checks reported station lift maintenance and avoids transfers at affected stations. An outage at your start or destination is flagged for staff assistance; it does not mean every lift at that station is unavailable. No reported outage is not a guarantee that all lifts work.

Step-free routes to bus stops, accessible station entrances and wheelchair-bay availability are not verified by these feeds. Bus access-time estimates use your selected walking or rolling pace. Everyone can adjust the train transfer allowance; enabling wheelchair access starts with at least eight minutes, which you can then change.
